3. After deploy: open mint later, also just sign

Correct order: (1) collection is already live on framlin.xyz, (2) owner signs setBaseURI, (3) owner signs setMintEnabled(true) — a separate signature, only when you decide to open mint, (4) only then can the public use mintFree, mintFreeFee and mintPaid.

Prices locked in the contract: Phase 1A free mint 0 BNB (first 300, max 3 per mint), Phase 1B free mint + 0.00015 BNB fee (#301–#1,000, max 3 per mint), Phase 2 paid mint 0.003 BNB per NFT (#1,001–#4,567, max 5 per mint), total supply 4,567, no treasury. Mint funds stay in the contract until the owner withdraws.
