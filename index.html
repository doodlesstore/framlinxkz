TWO PHASES. ONE SUPPLY.

Minting opens in stages from a single shared supply of 4,567 NFTs. When Phase 1 sells out, Phase 2 follows right after.

PHASE 01 FREE MINT
0 BNB / 0.00015 BNB

Phase 1A: the first 300 minters get the NFT free (max 3 per mint) — you only pay the BNB Chain network gas fee. Phase 1B: after 300 are gone, tokens #301 – #1,000 are still free NFTs, but with a 0.00015 BNB fee per NFT (max 3 per mint), until 1,000 are reached.

  • Allocation: 1,000 NFTs (tokens #1 – #1,000)
  • Phase 1A: #1 – #300, 0 BNB, max 3
  • Phase 1B: #301 – #1,000, 0.00015 BNB fee, max 3
  • Mint via AI Agent using the Free Mint / Fee Mint Prompt

0.003 BNB

  • Allocation: 3,567 NFTs (tokens #1,001 – #4,567)
  • Price: 0.003 BNB per NFT, max 5 per mint
  • Total payable = 0.003 × mint amount

FAQ.

What is the total supply of Agent Framlin?
The total is 4,567 NFTs: Phase 1 free mint 1,000 NFTs — #1–#300 at 0 BNB (max 3) and #301–#1,000 at a 0.00015 BNB fee (max 3) — and 3,567 paid mint NFTs in Phase 2 (tokens #1,001 – #4,567) at 0.003 BNB (max 5).
How do I mint?
All minting is done through an AI Agent. Paste your wallet address in the Mint via AI Agent section, choose a phase and amount, copy the matching prompt (Free Mint or Paid Mint), then send that prompt to your AI Agent. Check the transaction details and approve it in your own wallet.
When does Phase 2 open and what does it cost?
Phase 2 opens after the 1,000 free mints in Phase 1 are gone. The price stays fixed at 0.003 BNB per NFT (max 5 per mint), so the total you pay is 0.003 BNB multiplied by the number of NFTs you mint, plus the network gas fee.
Is the free mint really free?
The first 300 NFTs cost 0 BNB — you only pay the BNB Chain network gas fee. Tokens #301–#1,000 are still free NFTs, but with a 0.00015 BNB fee per NFT.
Is it safe to give the prompt to an AI Agent?
The prompt only contains mint instructions and your public wallet address. Never give your seed phrase or private key to anyone — a legitimate agent will always ask you to approve the transaction directly in your wallet.
